# Interface design | RFI 261001

Retain the LJMU and Performance Hub marks together; they identify the setting for this supplier demonstration. Identify OIV separately as its developer. The interface must not imply a University deployment, contract award or endorsement.

Use the existing navy and teal palette, legible dark text on light surfaces, and tabular numbers for financial amounts. Keep clinical, commercial and finance tasks distinct. Identify synthetic records, example policy settings and integration boundaries where a reviewer makes a decision.

Responsive tables scroll within their own containers. Controls need a visible name and keyboard focus. A completed local workflow, a visually inspected screen and formal accessibility conformance are different claims.

The white masthead, navy service navigation and condensed desk headings follow the School of Sport and Exercise Sciences. The Performance Hub informs the introductory serif heading and the single laboratory image. Working views use compact, plain action labels and group information by task. The photograph depicts a fictional exercise physiology scene created for this demonstration.

Roboto Condensed and Source Serif 4 are served from the repository with their SIL Open Font Licenses in `assets/fonts`. The [University brand guide](https://embed-rech-01.dialog.cm/ljmu/docs/ljmu_brand_guidelines_2025), the [School](https://www.ljmu.ac.uk/about-us/faculties/school-of-sport-and-exercise-sciences) and the [Performance Hub](https://www.ljmuperformancehub.com/) informed this independent interface.
