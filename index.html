About this demonstration

OIV created this demonstration to accompany its response to LJMU’s market engagement. It gives the University a way to try the proposed clinical and faculty workflows.

Teaching clinics

Reserve a shared room and equipment, record student activity and use the supervisor role to review and sign a draft.

Faculty services

Build an organisation’s order, approve it in a separate role and reconcile the illustrative invoice against its journal.

Use fictional information only. For implementation, OIV would configure permissions, clinical templates and finance mappings against LJMU’s agreed policies and test systems.

Reviewer guide

Start with a teaching appointment or follow an external client’s order. The role selector changes which actions you can take.

Bookings §3.3–3.4
Try an unavailable asset, then reserve an available space. Use the appointment list to change or cancel the reservation.
Clinical records §3.5–3.6
Save a note as a student and switch to the supervising clinician to countersign it.
Orders §3.8
Add a service, submit a purchase order as the client booker and approve it as the lab manager.
Finance §3.8–3.9
Match the approved order reference and total to the downloaded journal.

For remote follow-up (§3.7), try the linked patient invitation, consent and attendance record. The written response sets out the proposed video service, implementation, charges and terms.
