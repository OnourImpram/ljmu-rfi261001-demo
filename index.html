OIV Platform Architecture: The 6 Named Capability Pillars

Engineered explicitly for Liverpool John Moores University RFI 261001, OIV's Unified Clinical & Faculty Operations Platform is structured around six cohesive capability pillars spanning clinical education, physiological research, and commercial governance:

Pillar 1 (RFI 3.5 & 3.6)

Clinical Teaching & Supervision Hub

Relationship-Based Access Control (ReBAC) via OpenFGA (Zanzibar-style relationship model). Bounded student placement access, mandatory Student Draft → Practice Educator Diff Review → Cryptographic SHA-256 Countersignature, and student competency logbooks aligned with AHCS, HCPC, CSP, GOsC, and CASES.

Pillar 2 (RFI 3.5 & 3.6)

Allied Health & Sport Science Outcomes Studio

Standardised repeated clinical scoring across 5 disciplines (Physiotherapy MSK-HQ / VISA-P, Cardiovascular resting ECG intervals & validated risk stratification, CPET peak VO2 & ventilatory thresholds, and ForceDecks jump asymmetry). Multi-session exercise therapy plan templates.

Pillar 3 (RFI 3.3 & 3.4)

Unified Resource Dependency & Equipment Lifecycle Engine

Simultaneous 4-constraint satisfaction (Practitioner + Room/Bay + Equipment + configurable supervisor ratio, 1:4 in this demo). Full equipment calibration lifecycle tracking with automatic maintenance lockout routing to the Operational Exception Queue.

Pillar 4 (RFI 3.3 & 3.8)

Corporate CPD & Performance Hub Commerce Portal

Self-service B2C patient booking and B2B corporate squad testing checkout. Net 30 Purchase Order approval workflow, mixed-basket UK VAT calculation (exempt and 20% standard-rated), and automated CPD certificate generation upon verified attendance.

Pillar 5 (RFI 3.6 & 3.9)

Faculty Intelligence & De-Identified Research Studio

Presidio-based de-identification pipeline with outward postcode truncation (L3) and 5-year age-banding. Exportable research datasets (CSV/JSON) for SPSS/R, plus Synthea-based synthetic cohorts for a permanent Student Training Sandbox.

Pillar 6 (RFI 3.3, 3.4 & 3.8)

Operational Exception & Follow-Up Console

Single-pane operational console tracking: overdue student countersignatures (SLA auto-escalation), unmatched Oracle EBS bank transfers, equipment calibration locks, and failed patient notifications or pre-test questionnaires.
